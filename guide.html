Slide style guide

One set of rules for every DekoVägi slide, for people and for any AI model. Measured from 17 past decks (205 slides; 16 PowerPoint files checked element by element) and unified where they disagreed. The same numbers drive the slide builder, the template and the automatic check. Units: pt on the 1215 × 810 pt slide (× 4/3 for px at 1620 × 1080).

1. Slide and grid

Margins 78 pt left and right, 72 pt top; content 1059 pt wide; nothing but the logo below 724 pt. 2-up / 3-up / 4-up / 6-up widths: 517.5 / 337 / 246.75 / 156.5 pt. Text never starts left of x 78 or ends right of x 1137, except text inside a colour panel or card, which keeps 48 pt padding. Photos may bleed to any slide edge. Nothing but the logo sits below y 724.

4. Kicker block and title pattern

Ruum
Sissepääs
Punane vaip
The gold logo leaf, the kicker label, the title 22 pt below, the body 30 pt below the title. Plain words first, the last words or the second line in italic accent.

The leaf comes from the DekoVägi logo, filled gold, 16.5 pt tall and raised 2.25 pt above the label centre. The same leaf on every kicker, cover and section opener included. Chosen from 10 options.

6. Images

3:22:31:14:33:44:516:921:9
  • Every image slot uses one of the listed ratios; the gallery states the ratio per slot. Collage cells are free (they fill a grid).
  • A rendered final (photo, render, collage cell) fills its slot edge to edge (cover crop); a cut-out object sits on the background inside its box (contain).
  • Photos bleed to the slide edge or sit on the grid; never floating at random positions.
  • No rounded corners, borders or drop shadows on photos, except where a layout defines it: circle (D22, D43), arch (D40), and a 9 pt background-colour edge on a photo that overlaps another (D48, D62, D20). Cut-outs: no drop shadow.
  • Collage gaps: 6 pt in the background colour.
  • Minimum source size: 1.5 x the slot in pt (a full-bleed photo needs at least 1800 x 1200 px).
